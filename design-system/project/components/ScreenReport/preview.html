The answerHigh confidence

It is not. The Wall cannot be seen with the naked eye from orbit, and the belief is older than spaceflight.

No astronaut has reported seeing the Great Wall unaided from low Earth orbitC-031C-027. At 400 km the Wall, mostly under 10 m wide, is far below what the eye can resolveC-052. The claim appeared in print by 1932, decades before anyone could test itC-009.
